What We Collect

Quillory collects only the information necessary to provide you with a seamless journaling experience:

  • Account Information: Email address and password (encrypted)
  • Voice Recordings: Audio files you choose to record
  • Generated Summaries: AI-processed text summaries of your recordings
  • Usage Data: Basic analytics to improve app performance (anonymized)

How We Use Your Data

Your data serves one purpose: to provide you with the best journaling experience.

  • Voice Recordings: Processed by AI to generate summaries, then stored securely in your account
  • Summaries: Displayed in your app for reflection and review
  • Account Data: Used for authentication and account management
  • Analytics: Anonymized data helps us improve features and fix bugs

We never: Sell your data, share it with third parties for marketing, or use it for purposes beyond providing our service.

Your Rights & Control

You have complete control over your data:

  • Access: View all your recordings and summaries anytime
  • Export: Download your data in standard formats
  • Delete: Remove individual entries or your entire account
  • Modify: Edit or update your account information
  • Opt-out: Disable analytics tracking in settings

Data Security

We implement industry-standard security measures:

  • End-to-end encryption for all data transmission
  • Encrypted storage for recordings and summaries
  • Secure authentication with password hashing
  • Regular security audits and updates
  • GDPR and CCPA compliant data practices

AI Content Disclaimer

Summaries generated by Quillory's AI are intended for personal reflection and self-awareness. They should not be considered:

  • Medical or mental health advice
  • Professional counseling or therapy
  • Legal or financial guidance
  • A substitute for professional consultation

If you need professional support, please consult a qualified expert.

Data Retention

Your data is stored as long as your account is active. When you delete your account, all associated data (recordings, summaries, and account information) is permanently removed from our servers within 30 days.

Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy periodically. Significant changes will be communicated via email or in-app notification. Continued use of Quillory after updates constitutes acceptance of the new policy.
